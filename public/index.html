Sign in to copy playlists

Each account connects its own Deezer, and playlists are only ever written to the Deezer account you connect here. Google is used for the sign-in only — nothing is read from your Google account beyond your name, email and picture.

Public playlists only. Spotify’s own editorial and “Made for you” playlists (Discover Weekly, Release Radar) are not readable by third-party apps.

In your normal browser, signed in to deezer.com: press F12 → Application → Cookies → https://www.deezer.com → copy the value of arl (a long string, ~190 characters) and paste it here. It is checked against Deezer before being kept. It is your Deezer session — treat it like a password.

Asks Deezer’s own Track Mix about up to 25 songs of the playlist and suggests what most of them lead to — nothing that is already in it, and no more than a couple of songs per artist. Nothing is added until you press the button under the list.
