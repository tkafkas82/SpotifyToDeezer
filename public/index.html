Public playlists only. Spotify’s own editorial and “Made for you” playlists (Discover Weekly, Release Radar) are not readable by third-party apps.

In your normal browser, signed in to deezer.com: press F12 → Application → Cookies → https://www.deezer.com → copy the value of arl (a long string, ~190 characters) and paste it here. It is checked against Deezer before being kept, and stored only in data/gw.json on this machine. It is your Deezer session — treat it like a password.

Deezer will not complete a login inside an automated browser — its form silently re-renders instead of signing you in — so the Connect Deezer button up there is a long shot. This cookie is the reliable way in. App credentials in .env also work if you would rather register an app.
